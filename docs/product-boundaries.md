# Lemma landing — Product boundaries

## Purpose

Lemma is an AI agent harness for B2B revenue. It helps organizations identify
growth opportunities, move customer relationships forward, and increase
revenue.

This repository is the public landing surface. It may explain the product but
must not turn direction, experiments, or future plans into claims about live
capabilities.

## Boundaries for copy and customer-facing flows

- State only capabilities supported by the active product and its configuration.
  If that evidence is unavailable, present the statement as direction or ask
  for a product decision; do not infer it.
- Lemma works within user-defined goals and boundaries. Do not describe
  autonomous action outside those boundaries.
- Relevance, relationship context, and user trust matter more than sending
  message volume. Do not position the product as a spam or volume tool.
- The current product may use specialized external systems; do not claim Lemma
  replaces every CRM, enrichment, or communication tool.
- A future vision is not a shipped promise. Distinguish it clearly from what a
  customer can use today.

## Resolving uncertainty

For live behavior, the authority order is: active production code and
configuration, observed production behavior, then validated product
documentation. When sources conflict, surface the discrepancy. Missing context
does not authorize a new product claim.
